Meet Haley

A friend in your bridal party.

"My goal is to enhance your natural beauty, there's absolutely no masking here."

With over a decade of experience in the wedding industry and training with The House of Dior in London, Haley creates natural, timeless, radiant makeup that leaves you feeling confident, relaxed and completely yourself on the morning of your wedding.

She offers a bespoke, luxurious makeup package, an extensive luxury kit, and a warm, genuine approach. Think of her as an extended part of your bridal party, there from your first enquiry to the moment before you walk down the aisle.
