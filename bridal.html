Questions

Bridal FAQs.

How far ahead should I book?

Wedding dates are popular, so the sooner you enquire the better. Get in touch with your date and venue and Haley will let you know if she's available.

Can you do makeup for my bridesmaids and family?

Yes. Haley offers makeup for the whole bridal party. Let her know how many people you'd like looked after and she'll plan the morning around your schedule.

Where do you travel to?

Haley is based in West Bridgford, Nottingham and covers Nottinghamshire, Leicestershire, Rutland, Derbyshire and surrounding areas.

What if I don't usually wear much makeup?

That's absolutely fine. Haley's style is all about enhancing your natural beauty, never masking it. Your look will feel like you.

Can I have a makeup lesson instead?

Of course. One-to-one lessons are available for brides who'd like to learn how to recreate a look themselves. See makeup lessons.
